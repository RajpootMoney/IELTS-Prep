# IELTS Examination Time Management & Test-Day Protocol

Poor time management can reduce a Band 7.5 capable candidate to Band 6.0. In IELTS, time management is not an afterthought; it is an active test component.

---

## 1. Reading Time Management (The 15-20-25 Rule)

You have **60 minutes** for 40 questions. Remember: **There is NO transfer time in GT Reading!**

```
Minute 00 - 15: Section 1 (Passages 1 & 2)  --> Target: 14 Questions
Minute 15 - 35: Section 2 (Workplace Texts) --> Target: 13 Questions
Minute 35 - 55: Section 3 (Extended Text)   --> Target: 13 Questions
Minute 55 - 60: Verification & Direct Check --> Fix blanks, verify word count
```

### The 90-Second Rule
If you cannot find the answer to a reading question within **90 seconds**:
1. Place a light pencil dot or note in the margin.
2. Select an educated guess (never leave a blank!).
3. Move immediately to the next question.
4. Return only during the final 5-minute review block.
*Spending 4 minutes wrestling with one question in Section 1 often means losing 3 easy questions in Section 3.*

---

## 2. Writing Time Management (The 20 / 40 Minute Split)

Always follow this exact time distribution:

### Task 1 (Letter — 20 Minutes)
- **00:00 – 03:00 (3 mins): Planning**
  - Identify tone (Formal, Semi-formal, Informal).
  - Draft bullet points: 2 specific circumstantial details for each of the 3 prompts.
- **03:00 – 17:00 (14 mins): Writing**
  - Salutation + Opening purpose (1 para).
  - Bullet 1 (1 para).
  - Bullet 2 (1 para).
  - Bullet 3 (1 para).
  - Sign-off (1 line).
- **17:00 – 20:00 (3 mins): Editing**
  - Check spelling, singular/plural nouns, and verb tenses.

### Task 2 (Essay — 40 Minutes)
- **00:00 – 07:00 (7 mins): Strategic Planning**
  - Deconstruct prompt: underline keywords and micro-topics.
  - Determine stance / thesis.
  - Brainstorm 2 solid arguments with realistic examples.
- **07:00 – 35:00 (28 mins): Writing**
  - Introduction: 5 mins.
  - Body Paragraph 1: 10 mins.
  - Body Paragraph 2: 10 mins.
  - Conclusion: 3 mins.
- **35:00 – 40:00 (5 mins): Meticulous Proofreading**
  - Scan for repeated words, missing articles (*the, a, an*), punctuation, and clear paragraph breaks.

---

## 3. Listening Focus & Concentration Control

In Listening, you hear the recording **only once**.
- **The Pre-Section Window (30–45s):** Read questions and predict gaps.
- **During Audio Play:** Keep one hand on the pencil/keyboard; track current question and upcoming question simultaneously.
- **The 30-Second Post-Section Window:** Do NOT sit back. Use this time to read the NEXT section's questions immediately.
- **The 10-Minute Transfer Time (Paper-Based Only):** Check spelling, capital letters for proper nouns, and numerical formats. *(On Computer-Delivered IELTS, you receive only 2 minutes at the end).*

---

## 4. Test Day Mental Endurance Routine
1. **Sleep & Hydration:** Adequate rest 48 hours prior; do not dehydrate, but avoid excessive caffeine that spikes anxiety.
2. **Accept Imperfection:** If an answer in Listening slips away, let it go instantly. Missing 2 questions still easily secures **Band 8.5**; panicking over 1 question causes a 5-question cascade failure.
