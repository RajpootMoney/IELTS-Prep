# IELTS General Training Writing Strategies: Achieving Band 7.0+

Writing is often the stumbling block for Canadian PR candidates. To score **Band 7.0**, you must satisfy all four assessment criteria at a consistent Band 7 level across both tasks:

```
Total Writing Score = (Task 1 × 1/3) + (Task 2 × 2/3)
```

---

## 1. Task 1: The General Training Letter Mastery

You have **20 minutes** to write a minimum of **150 words**.

### Tone & Register Blueprint

| Tone | Context & Recipient | Salutation | Sign-off | Style Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Formal** | Companies, officials, employers, unknown recipients | *Dear Sir or Madam,* (or *Dear Mr. Davis,*) | *Yours faithfully,* (or *Yours sincerely,*) | **No contractions** (*do not*, not *don't*). Formal vocabulary (*inquire*, *commence*, *regret*). |
| **Semi-Formal**| Landlords, neighbors, colleagues, course supervisors | *Dear Mr. Jenkins,* / *Dear Sarah,* | *Yours sincerely,* / *Warm regards,* | Polite, respectful, direct. Limited contractions. Professional yet personal. |
| **Informal** | Close friends, family members | *Dear Alex,* | *Best wishes,* / *Warmly,* / *All the best,* | Contractions encouraged (*I've*, *can't*). Conversational idioms, emotive expressions. |

### The 3-Bullet Point Rule
The prompt will always give you three bullet points. 
> **Examiner Trap:** If you write a brilliant response to Bullet 1 and Bullet 2, but write only one hasty sentence for Bullet 3, **Task Achievement is capped at Band 6.0**.
- Allocate **one distinct paragraph per bullet point**.
- Develop each bullet point with plausible specific details (dates, names, reasons, exact requests).

## 2. Task 2: The 4-Paragraph Band 7 Essay Architecture

You have **40 minutes** to write a minimum of **250 words** (aim for 270–290 words).

```
Paragraph 1: Introduction (40–45 words)
├── Sentence 1: Paraphrase the prompt topic using synonyms
└── Sentence 2: Direct Thesis Statement (State your clear position)

Paragraph 2: First Main Argument (PEEL Method, 90–100 words)
├── Point: Strong topic sentence stating the primary argument
├── Explanation: Explain why/how this occurs
├── Example: Realistic, specific illustrative example
└── Link: Summarize impact on the main thesis

Paragraph 3: Second Main Argument / Counter-Perspective (PEEL Method, 90–100 words)
├── Point: Strong topic sentence introducing the second perspective
├── Explanation: In-depth reasoning
├── Example: Realistic evidence or scenario
└── Link: Synthesize back to your core position

Paragraph 4: Conclusion (35–45 words)
├── Sentence 1: Re-state thesis using varied vocabulary
└── Sentence 2: Final summarizing thought / future outlook (No new arguments!)
```

---

## 3. The PEEL Paragraph Framework

To achieve Band 7+ in **Task Response** and **Coherence & Cohesion**, every body paragraph must follow **PEEL**:

- **P (Point):** State the single central idea in a crisp topic sentence.
  - *Example:* "One significant benefit of remote work is the reduction in employee commuting stress."
- **E (Explanation):** Elaborate on why this is true.
  - *Example:* "Eliminating lengthy daily journeys allows professionals to reclaim valuable hours, thereby fostering better work-life balance and mental well-being."
- **E (Example):** Provide a grounded illustration.
  - *Example:* "For instance, a recent survey conducted among Canadian tech workers revealed that employees saved approximately ten hours weekly by working from home."
- **L (Link):** Connect the example back to the question.
  - *Example:* "Consequently, this saved time directly translates into enhanced focus and sustained daily productivity."

---

## 4. Upgrading from Band 6 to Band 7 Grammar

Band 6 writers write mostly simple and compound sentences (*and, but, so*), with frequent small errors in articles and prepositions. Band 7 writers use a **variety of complex structures with high accuracy**.

| Band 6 Pattern | Band 7 Upgraded Structure |
|---|---|
| *People work from home. They have more free time. They can exercise.* | **Complex Sentence:** *"Because employees work from home, they have greater flexibility, which enables them to exercise regularly."* |
| *If companies do not train staff, they will fail.* | **Conditional with Inversion:** *"Were organizations to neglect staff training, their long-term competitiveness would be severely jeopardized."* |
| *Pollution is a big problem and cities must fix it.* | **Passive Voice with Relative Clause:** *"Urban environmental degradation, which is largely driven by vehicular emissions, must be addressed urgently by municipal authorities."* |
