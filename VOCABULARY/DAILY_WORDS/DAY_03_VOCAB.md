# IELTS GT Vocabulary Bank — Day 03
### Theme: Workplace, Employment, Contracts & Professional Life (GT Section 2)

Study these 10 targeted words. Memorize the collocations, Hinglish nuance, and IELTS exam usage.

---

## 1. Entitlement *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The amount to which a person has a right; the official right to do or have something.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** हकदारी, अधिकार (Haqdari, adhikar)
- **Collocations:** `annual leave entitlement, benefit entitlement, statutory entitlement`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Full-time employees have an entitlement to 25 days of paid annual leave."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** GT Reading Section 2 workplace contracts and staff handbook passages.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Right' is general; 'entitlement' refers to a specific, quantified legal allowance.

---

## 2. Remuneration *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** Money paid for work or a service; financial compensation.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** पारिश्रमिक, वेतन (Parishramik, vetan)
- **Collocations:** `remuneration package, adequate remuneration, financial remuneration`
- **Example Sentence:** *"The executive remuneration package consists of a base salary, performance bonuses, and stock options."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** High-scoring academic synonym for 'salary' or 'pay' in Task 2 and GT Reading.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Formal. 'Salary' is fixed monthly pay; 'remuneration' includes all benefits, perks, and bonuses.

---

## 3. Appraisal *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** An act of assessing something or someone, especially an employee's performance.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** मूल्यांकन (Mulyankan)
- **Collocations:** `performance appraisal, annual appraisal, appraisal interview`
- **Example Sentence:** *"During her annual appraisal, Maria discussed her professional development goals with her supervisor."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Standard workplace topic in GT Reading Section 2 and Speaking Part 1 & 2.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Evaluation' is broad. 'Appraisal' specifically denotes a formal workplace performance review.

---

## 4. Grievance *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** A real or imagined cause for complaint, especially unfair treatment.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** शिकायत, व्यथा (Shikayat, vyatha)
- **Collocations:** `file a grievance, grievance procedure, legitimate grievance`
- **Example Sentence:** *"If an employee feels unfairly treated, they are advised to initiate the company's formal grievance procedure."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Key term in GT Reading Section 2 dispute-resolution texts and Task 1 workplace complaint letters.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Complaint' is an act of complaining. 'Grievance' is the formal, documented wrong.

---

## 5. Probationary *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Relating to a period of testing or trial before someone is admitted to full status.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** परिवीक्षाधीन, ट्रायल का समय (Parivikshadhin, trial period)
- **Collocations:** `probationary period, probationary employee, six-month probationary contract`
- **Example Sentence:** *"During the three-month probationary period, employment may be terminated with one week's notice."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Universal term in GT Reading workplace agreements.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Do not confuse with 'temporary'; a probationary worker is in line for permanent status if approved.

---

## 6. Redundancy *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The state of being no longer in employment because there is no more work available.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** छंटनी (Chhantani)
- **Collocations:** `voluntary redundancy, redundancy package, face redundancy`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Following corporate restructuring, fifty administrative workers were offered voluntary redundancy."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** British English legal term used in IELTS tests. North American equivalent is 'layoff'.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Redundancy means the job itself has ceased to exist; it is NOT termination for poor performance.

---

## 7. Compliance *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The state or fact of according with or meeting rules or standards.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** अनुपालन (Anupalan - niyamon ka palan)
- **Collocations:** `regulatory compliance, in compliance with, ensure compliance`
- **Example Sentence:** *"All factory managers must ensure strict compliance with federal occupational health regulations."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** GT Reading workplace health & safety regulations.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Associated with law, policy, and safety standards.

---

## 8. Discretion *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The freedom to decide what should be done in a particular situation.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** विवेकाधिकार, अपनी मर्जी से निर्णय (Vivekadhikar)
- **Collocations:** `at the manager's discretion, sole discretion, exercise discretion`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Granting unpaid compassionate leave remains entirely at the employer's discretion."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Tricky reading distractor in GT contracts (means it is not guaranteed by right!).
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Choice' is casual. 'Discretion' signifies authorized decision-making power.

---

## 9. Incentive *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** A thing that motivates or encourages someone to do something.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** प्रोत्साहन, प्रेरक (Protsahan)
- **Collocations:** `financial incentive, powerful incentive, tax incentive`
- **Example Sentence:** *"The firm introduced performance-linked bonuses as an incentive for sales personnel."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Frequent in Task 2 essays discussing workplace motivation and education policy.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** An incentive encourages action before the fact; a reward is given after completion.

---

## 10. Occupational *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Relating to a job or profession.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** व्यावसायिक, काम से संबंधित (Vyavasayik)
- **Collocations:** `occupational hazard, occupational therapy, occupational health`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Repetitive strain injury is recognized as a serious occupational hazard among computer programmers."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** High-frequency in GT Reading Section 2 health and safety documents.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Formal synonym for 'job-related' or 'work-related'.
