# IELTS GT Vocabulary Bank — Day 02
### Theme: Accommodation, Housing, Tenancy & Local Amenities (Section 1 L & R)

Study these 10 targeted words. Memorize the collocations, Hinglish nuance, and IELTS exam usage.

---

## 1. Tenancy *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** Possession of land or property as a tenant; the period of occupying property.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** किरायेदारी (Kirayedari)
- **Collocations:** `tenancy agreement, period of tenancy, terminate a tenancy`
- **Example Sentence:** *"According to clause 4 of the tenancy agreement, notice must be served one calendar month in advance."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** GT Reading Section 1 & 2 lease contracts, and Task 1 letters to landlords.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Rent' is the payment; 'tenancy' is the legal status and relationship of occupying.

---

## 2. Furnished *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Available with furniture already provided.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** फर्नीचर सहित (Furniture sahit)
- **Collocations:** `fully furnished, semi-furnished, unfurnished apartment`
- **Example Sentence:** *"The rental listing specified that the flat is fully furnished, including white goods in the kitchen."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Section 1 Listening form completion (note British term 'white goods' = fridge, washing machine).
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Equipped' usually refers to machines/tools; 'furnished' refers to living furniture.

---

## 3. Deposit *(noun / verb)*
- **English Meaning:** A sum of money paid as a first installment or security against damage.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** जमानत राशि, बयाना (Zamanat rashi)
- **Collocations:** `refundable deposit, security deposit, pay a deposit`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Tenants are required to lodge a security deposit equivalent to six weeks of rent."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Frequent listening answer in Section 1 (e.g. 'How much deposit is required?').
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Distinguish between 'holding deposit' (to reserve) and 'security deposit' (against damages).

---

## 4. Refurbish *(verb)*
- **English Meaning:** To renovate and redecorate something, especially a building.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** नवीनीकरण करना, मरम्मत कर नया रूप देना (Navinikaran karna)
- **Collocations:** `recently refurbished, refurbish the premises, extensive refurbishment`
- **Example Sentence:** *"The community sports hall was closed throughout March while the gymnasium was refurbished."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** High frequency in Listening Section 2 (talks on local community centers) and GT Reading Section 1.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Repair' means fixing something broken. 'Refurbish' means upgrading aesthetic and structural quality.

---

## 5. Proximity *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** Nearness in space, time, or relationship.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** नजदीकी, निकटता (Najdeeki, nikat-ta)
- **Collocations:** `close proximity to, in the immediate proximity, proximity of public transit`
- **Example Sentence:** *"One major selling point of the residential complex is its close proximity to the metro station."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Essential in GT Reading advertisements and Task 1 letters describing location advantages.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Used with 'to' ('in proximity to', never 'proximity of' in that sense). Much more formal than 'near'.

---

## 6. Spacious *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Having ample space; roomy.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** खुला, विशाल, हवादार (Khula, vishaal)
- **Collocations:** `spacious layout, spacious living room, remarkably spacious`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Although the cottage looks compact from the exterior, it offers a remarkably spacious interior."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Listening Section 1 & Speaking Part 1 when describing accommodation.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Big' is bland; 'spacious' indicates pleasant, uncluttered open living space.

---

## 7. Utilities *(noun (plural))*
- **English Meaning:** Basic public services supplied to a property, such as electricity, gas, water, and sewerage.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** बुनियादी जनसुविधाएं (बिजली, पानी, गैस) (Bijli, paani, gas)
- **Collocations:** `utilities included, monthly utility bills, public utilities`
- **Example Sentence:** *"The monthly rent is $1,400, but tenants are responsible for paying their own utilities."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Constant distractor in Section 1 listening (checking whether rent includes utilities or not!).
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Do not confuse with 'facilities' (gym, pool, parking). Utilities are core services.

---

## 8. Premises *(noun (plural))*
- **English Meaning:** A house or building, together with its land and outbuildings, occupied by a business or person.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** परिसर, भवन (Parisar, bhavan)
- **Collocations:** `on the premises, business premises, vacate the premises`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Smoking is strictly prohibited anywhere on the premises, including the outdoor courtyards."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** GT Reading Section 1 & 2 safety notices and office regulations.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Always plural ('the premises are', not 'the premises is'). Refers to the physical grounds.

---

## 9. Landlord / Landlady *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** A person who rents land, a building, or an accommodation to a tenant.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** मकान मालिक / मालकिन (Makaan maalik)
- **Collocations:** `private landlord, dispute with the landlord, notify the landlord`
- **Example Sentence:** *"I am writing to formally notify my landlord regarding the persistent water leak in the master bathroom."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Primary recipient in Task 1 semi-formal complaint or maintenance letters.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** In legal documents, often referred to as the 'lessor'.

---

## 10. Disruption *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** Disturbance or problems which interrupt an event, activity, or process.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** बाधा, रुकावट (Baadha, rukawat)
- **Collocations:** `cause disruption, minimize disruption, service disruption`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Commuters experienced significant travel disruption following unexpected track maintenance."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Frequent in GT Reading timetables and Task 1 letters requesting compensation.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Delay' means taking longer; 'disruption' means complete upset or interruption of normal flow.
