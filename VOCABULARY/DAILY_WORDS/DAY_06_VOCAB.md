# IELTS GT Vocabulary Bank — Day 06
### Theme: Societal Trends, Technology & Work-Life Balance (Task 2 & Speaking Part 3)

Study these 10 targeted words. Memorize the collocations, Hinglish nuance, and IELTS exam usage.

---

## 1. Proliferation *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** Rapid increase in the number or amount of something.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** प्रसार, तेजी से वृद्धि (Prasaar, tezi se vriddhi)
- **Collocations:** `proliferation of smartphones, nuclear proliferation, rapid proliferation`
- **Example Sentence:** *"The proliferation of digital communication tools has permanently altered traditional workplace hierarchies."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Band 8+ noun in Task 2 introductions to replace 'many people now use'.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Implies explosive, uncontrolled multiplication.

---

## 2. Automation *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The use of largely automatic equipment in a system of manufacturing or other production process.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** स्वचालन (Svachalan - machines doing human work)
- **Collocations:** `workplace automation, industrial automation, rise of automation`
- **Example Sentence:** *"While automation boosts corporate efficiency, it threatens routine administrative employment."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Core theme in Canadian PR essays regarding future labor markets.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Mechanization' is using physical machines; 'automation' involves software/AI performing decisions.

---

## 3. Indispensable *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Absolutely necessary; impossible to be without.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** अपरिहार्य, जिसके बिना काम न चले (Apariharya)
- **Collocations:** `indispensable tool, prove indispensable, indispensable part of life`
- **Example Sentence:** *"High-speed internet has evolved from a luxury into an indispensable utility for remote workers."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Upgraded Band 7+ replacement for 'very important' or 'essential'.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Spelling trap: note ending is '-able', not '-ible'!

---

## 4. Preoccupied *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Engrossed in thought; distracted.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** चिंतित, किसी विचार में खोया हुआ (Chintit, khoya hua)
- **Collocations:** `preoccupied with work, increasingly preoccupied, mentally preoccupied`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Modern professionals are often so preoccupied with career advancement that they neglect physical health."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Speaking Part 1, 2, and 3 when discussing stress and lifestyles.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Used with preposition 'with'.

---

## 5. Obsolete *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** No longer produced or used; out of date.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** अप्रचलित, पुराना (Aprachalit, purana)
- **Collocations:** `render obsolete, become obsolete, virtually obsolete`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Generative artificial intelligence may render several entry-level coding tasks obsolete."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Task 2 technology topics and Reading Section 3.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Old' is chronological age; 'obsolete' means useless because a superior replacement exists.

---

## 6. Sedentary *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Tending to spend much time seated; somewhat inactive.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** गतिहीन, बैठे रहने वाला (Gatiheen, baithe rehne wala)
- **Collocations:** `sedentary lifestyle, sedentary job, sedentary habits`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Adopting a sedentary lifestyle dramatically increases susceptibility to cardiovascular disease."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** High-scoring collocation for health, lifestyle, and work essays.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Specifically denotes physical stillness or seated posture.

---

## 7. Cohesion *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The action or fact of forming a united whole; togetherness.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** एकजुटता, सामंजस्य (Ekjutta, samanjasya)
- **Collocations:** `social cohesion, community cohesion, team cohesion`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Community cultural festivals play an essential role in fostering social cohesion among diverse immigrant populations."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Vital for societal essays in Task 2 and Speaking Part 3.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Also an official IELTS criteria term ('Coherence and Cohesion').

---

## 8. Autonomy *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The right or condition of self-government; freedom from external control or influence.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** स्वायत्तता, स्वतंत्रता (Swayattata, swatantrata)
- **Collocations:** `greater autonomy, individual autonomy, workplace autonomy`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Employees who enjoy schedule autonomy report higher job satisfaction and lower attrition rates."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Workplace and management topics in Task 2 and GT Reading.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Independence' is general; 'autonomy' specifically means having the authority to make one's own choices.

---

## 9. Isolation *(noun)*
- **English Meaning:** The state of being separated from other people or things.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** अलगाव, एकांत (Algaav, ekaant)
- **Collocations:** `social isolation, feelings of isolation, geographical isolation`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Without regular team interactions, prolonged remote work can inadvertently induce feelings of isolation."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Common in Task 2 essays on remote work, aging populations, and urbanization.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** 'Solitude' is peaceful being alone; 'isolation' carries a negative, disconnected connotation.

---

## 10. Disproportionate *(adjective)*
- **English Meaning:** Too large or too small in comparison with something else.
- **Hindi / Hinglish Nuance:** असंगत, अनुपातहीन (Asangat, anupaat-heen)
- **Collocations:** `disproportionate impact, disproportionate amount, disproportionately affected`
- **Example Sentence:** *"Rising public transit fares impose a disproportionate financial burden on low-income households."*
- **IELTS Exam Application:** Essential modifier for Task 2 socio-economic analysis.
- **Trap / Nuance Distinction:** Adverb form 'disproportionately' is exceptional for demonstrating Band 7+ grammatical range.
