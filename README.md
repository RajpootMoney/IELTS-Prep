## 2. Target Score Breakdown: The CLB 9/10 Reality

For Canadian Express Entry, achieving **CLB 9** (Canadian Language Benchmark) unlocks the maximum **Skill Transferability bonus (+50 points)** in the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS). Missing even one module by 0.5 can cost 50+ CRS points.

| Module | Target Band | Minimum Raw Score Required | Official Difficulty Notes |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Listening** | **8.0** | **35–36 / 40** | Highest priority. Requires precision in Section 4 monologues and 100% accuracy on Section 1 & 2 basics. Only 4–5 mistakes allowed in total. |
| **Reading (GT)** | **7.0** | **34–35 / 40** | **Critical Difference:** GT Reading requires **34/40** for Band 7.0 (Academic requires only 30/40). A score of 30/40 in GT Reading is only Band 6.0! |
| **Writing** | **7.0** | **Band 7 in both tasks** | Task 1 Letter (1/3 weight, 150+ words) + Task 2 Essay (2/3 weight, 250+ words). Evaluated on TA/TR, CC, LR, GRA. |
| **Speaking** | **7.0** | **Band 7 across parts** | Part 1 Interview + Part 2 Long Turn + Part 3 Discussion. Evaluated on Fluency, Lexical Resource, Grammar, Pronunciation. |

## 3. How to Use This System Daily

### Daily Study Routine (60–90 Minutes Regular Day)
1. **Open the Day Folder**: Navigate to `DAILY_ASSIGNMENTS/WEEK_XX/DAY_XX/`.
2. **Review Objectives & Strategy (5 mins)**: Read the daily objective, exam strategy, and targeted vocabulary.
3. **Attempt the Tasks (45–60 mins)**:
   - **Listening**: Open `Daily_Task.pdf`, play `audio_track.wav`. Write answers in `Answer_Sheet.pdf` without checking the answer key.
   - **Reading**: Complete the timed reading passage.
   - **Writing / Speaking**: Complete the scheduled writing task or prepare the speaking prompt.
4. **Self-Check or Submit for Evaluation (15–20 mins)**:
   - Compare your Listening & Reading answers with `Answer_Key.pdf`.
   - Submit your responses (including Writing text and Speaking responses) directly in our chat session.
   - I will conduct a line-by-line examiner assessment, explain why incorrect options were traps, and provide band scores.
5. **Log Mistakes in `ERROR_LOG.xlsx` (5 mins)**: Record every missed question, its category, and the specific root cause.

### Busy Day Protocol (30 Minutes Minimum Effective Dose)
On days when work demands 11+ hours:
- **Priority 1**: Listening section drill (15 mins) using `audio_track.wav`.
- **Priority 2**: Vocabulary & Strategy review (10 mins).
- **Priority 3**: Error Log quick review (5 mins).
*Do not skip days; momentum and ear-training are critical for Listening Band 8.*

## 4. The Error Classification Matrix

Every incorrect answer must be assigned one of the 10 official root-cause categories in `ERROR_LOG.xlsx`:

1. `VOCABULARY`: Did not know the word or phrase in the question or audio/text.
2. `PARAPHRASING`: Heard or saw the synonym but failed to connect it to the question keyword.
3. `DISTRACTOR`: Fell for a speaker self-correction or a reading distractor text.
4. `CONCENTRATION`: Lost focus during audio playback or long reading passage.
5. `TIME_MANAGEMENT`: Rushed, ran out of time, or spent too long on an earlier question.
6. `SPELLING_WORD_LIMIT`: Misspelled word or exceeded word count (e.g. wrote 2 words when limit was ONE).
7. `QUESTION_INTERPRETATION`: Misunderstood what the question was asking for.
8. `LOGIC_ASSUMPTION`: (Especially in TFNG) Brought outside knowledge rather than sticking strictly to the text.
9. `GRAMMATICAL_ACCURACY`: Incorrect tense, subject-verb agreement, or pluralization.
10. `STRUCTURE_IDEA`: (Writing/Speaking) Unclear topic sentence, lack of extension, or weak cohesion.
