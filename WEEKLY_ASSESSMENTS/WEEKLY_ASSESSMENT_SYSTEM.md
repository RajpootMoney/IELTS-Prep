# Weekly Assessment System & Milestone Reports

Every seven days, we conduct a structured performance audit to evaluate your progress against your Canadian PR target (Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0, Speaking 7.0).

---

## 1. Structure of the Weekly Assessment (Conducted on Day 7)

1. **Listening Mini-Mock (15 Questions):**
   - Section 1 (Form / Booking): 5 Questions
   - Section 2 / 3 (Community / Academic Project): 5 Questions
   - Section 4 (Academic Monologue): 5 Questions
   - *Target: Minimum 13 / 15 correct (86%+ accuracy).*

2. **Reading Mini-Mock (15 Questions):**
   - GT Section 1 (Practical texts & notices): 5 Questions
   - GT Section 2 (Workplace training / contracts): 5 Questions
   - GT Section 3 (Extended general-interest passage): 5 Questions
   - *Target: Minimum 13 / 15 correct (86%+ accuracy for GT Band 7.0).*

3. **Writing Evaluation (Full Timed Task 1 or Task 2):**
   - Task 1: 150+ words in 20 minutes (Letter).
   - Task 2: 250+ words in 40 minutes (Essay).
   - Evaluated under the 4 official criteria (TA/TR, CC, LR, GRA).

4. **Speaking Evaluation (Part 1, 2, and 3):**
   - 11–14 minute recorded interview or transcript analysis.
   - Evaluated under FC, LR, GRA, PR.

5. **Spaced Repetition Vocabulary Recall Audit:**
   - Active recall of the 70 target vocabulary items introduced during the week.
